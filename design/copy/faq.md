## Questions
1. **Do I need a utility bill or international passport to open a Duro wallet?**
   No. You only need your 11-digit National Identification Number (NIN), a Nigerian phone number, an email address, and a live selfie check.
2. **How does the automatic US Dollar split work when I get paid?**
   When your employer or a POS agent sends Naira to your Duro virtual account, Duro credits your Naira Spend balance and immediately converts your chosen percentage (15% by default) into US Dollars.
3. **What happens if my phone is stolen or someone forces me to sign in on a new device?**
   Signing in from an unrecognized device triggers an automatic 24-hour cooling-off lock. During those 24 hours, US Dollar savings cannot be withdrawn and new bank beneficiaries cannot be added—even with a selfie—while normal transfers to your pre-saved Trusted Accounts remain open.
4. **How can I pay rent in Naira without selling my US Dollar savings?**
   In the Credit & Statements tab, you can pledge your US Dollar balance as collateral (at 150% coverage) and receive an instant Naira advance directly into your Spend balance.
5. **What fees does Duro charge on bank transfers?**
   Sending Naira to any Nigerian bank costs a flat ₦200 transfer fee plus ₦15 VAT (₦215 total). The exact fee and total debit are shown in a confirmation modal before you approve the payout.
