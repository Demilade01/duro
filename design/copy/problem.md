# Problem Copy (`problem.md`)

- **Eyebrow**: `Why wages slip away`
- **Headline (2 lines max)**:
  Working hard in Naira shouldn't mean
  watching your savings shrink every quarter.
- **Subtitle (3 lines max)**:
  Salaried staff, riders, and freelancers in Nigeria face three roadblocks when trying to protect what they earn.
- **Points**:
  1. **Manual saving happens too late**: By the time bills and family transfers clear, nothing is left to convert into US Dollars.
  2. **Paperwork locks out everyday earners**: Dollar bank accounts ask for utility bills, referees, and high minimum balances.
  3. **Street-level phone theft drains accounts**: A stolen phone or forced transfer on a new device can wipe out months of savings in minutes.
