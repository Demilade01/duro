## Bento Cards
1. **Automatic Salary Split (Wide Card)**
   - **Title**: Save in US Dollars before you spend a kobo
   - **Body**: Receive your salary or POS agent deposit into a Providus Bank account. Duro keeps 85% in Naira Spend and moves 15% into US Dollar Save automatically.
2. **Transparent NIP Bank Payouts**
   - **Title**: Exact bank fees shown before you send
   - **Body**: Send Naira to any Nigerian bank with a clear quote upfront: ₦200 transfer fee plus ₦15 VAT (₦215 total) and a 4-stage NIP delivery tracker.
3. **Dollar-Backed Naira Credit & Statements**
   - **Title**: Borrow Naira for rent without selling your Dollars
   - **Body**: Export a verified cash-flow statement for Lagos landlords, or lock your US Dollars at 150% coverage to get an instant Naira advance.
4. **24-Hour Anti-Coercion Lock (Wide Card)**
   - **Title**: Built for lost phones and street-level safety
   - **Body**: Sign-ins on an unrecognized device freeze US Dollar withdrawals and new bank recipients for 24 hours—even under coercion—while keeping pre-saved Trusted Accounts open for family emergencies.
